# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The plugin serves Guyanese WooCommerce merchants who accept MMG payments and shoppers who may not know MMG's hosted checkout terminology. Merchants configure credentials, review payment state and support customers. Shoppers need to understand the next payment step quickly on desktop and mobile without learning technical terms.

## Product Purpose

MMG Checkout for WooCommerce connects a store's checkout and order lifecycle to MMG payment services. Success means that a customer can choose the correct MMG payment route, understand what to do, complete payment once and return to an order whose payment state was verified against MMG.

## Brand Personality

Clear, reliable and local. The interface should support trust, which means it should state what will happen, distinguish pending approval from completed payment and explain failures without exposing payment or credential data.

## Anti-references

Do not use large tutorials, decorative payment panels, unfamiliar controls, hidden state changes or instructions that repeat the payment method title. Do not imply that a request was paid before authenticated verification succeeds. Do not present public documentation examples as merchant credentials.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the customer in the payment task with short instructions and progressive disclosure.
2. Use familiar WooCommerce controls and preserve the store theme unless clarity or accessibility requires a scoped override.
3. State payment state precisely, especially when customer approval or MMG verification is still pending.
4. Keep credentials and provider requests on the server and display only the information the current user needs.
5. Design mobile-first because many MMG customers complete checkout and approval on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Customer guidance must support keyboard use, visible focus, meaningful alternative text, reduced motion and sufficient colour contrast. Controls should have suitable mobile touch targets. Instructions must not rely on colour, images or motion alone and the essential steps must remain available as text.
